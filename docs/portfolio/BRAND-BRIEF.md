# Chumbow Lab — Brand Brief

**Version:** Flagship portfolio v1.0  
**Account:** [github.com/chumbowlab](https://github.com/chumbowlab)  
**Portfolio:** [paulchumbow254.lovable.app](https://paulchumbow254.lovable.app)

## 1. Identity

**Chumbow Lab is the digital-work portfolio of Paul Chumbow.** It brings together founder and operations support with practical web delivery, WordPress implementation and workflow improvement.

**Identity sentence**

I help founders and small teams make day-to-day work clearer through dependable operations support, practical web solutions and thoughtfully designed digital workflows.

**Portfolio line**

Turning Ideas Into Systems That Work.

## 2. Audience

- Founders and executives who need dependable operational support.
- Small businesses and digital teams that need WordPress, website or technical support.
- Teams seeking practical ways to organise recurring work and reduce manual hand-offs.
- Recruiters hiring for technical executive assistance, digital operations, WordPress and workflow-support roles.

## 3. Positioning

The differentiator is an operations-first approach to digital delivery: understand the work and the people doing it, map the process, build or improve the simplest useful system, then document its capabilities and limitations.

This is a positioning choice, not a claim that no other provider offers similar services.

## 4. Message architecture

**Primary themes**
- Digital operations and founder support
- WordPress and website implementation
- Lightweight business tools and dashboards
- AI-assisted workflows and practical automation

**Primary search themes**
- WordPress support and development
- Website maintenance and SEO
- Digital operations support
- Founder and executive support
- Workflow automation for small teams
- Business operations dashboards
- Property maintenance tracking

**Conversational questions**
- How can a small team organise operational follow-ups?
- How can a property manager track maintenance requests?
- What should a founder operations dashboard include?
- How can a WordPress website be maintained and improved?

Use these terms naturally where they describe the actual work. Do not keyword-stuff profile or repository copy.

## 5. Opening hook

A lot of important work gets lost between messages, spreadsheets and follow-ups. Chumbow Lab focuses on making that work easier to see, organise and improve.

## 6. Proof inventory

| Claim or capability | Evidence available | Public-use rule |
|---|---|---|
| Property-maintenance workflow prototype | Public repository and working HTML source | Describe as a prototype with local browser storage |
| WordPress implementation | Public CMFI Kenya website and portfolio context | Describe the actual contribution; do not imply ownership of the ministry or its content |
| Founder and operations support | Work history supplied for professional materials | Keep dates, duties and scope consistent with the CV |
| AI-assisted workflow interest | Tools, training and project experiments | Do not claim autonomous production systems or measured efficiency gains without evidence |

## 7. Claims intentionally excluded

No invented clients, testimonials, awards, revenue, user counts, performance improvements, response-time promises, production deployment claims or guaranteed AI-search placements. A tool used during a project is not automatically a delivered automation service.

Use one primary action on the profile and each project README. The portfolio and LinkedIn are supporting context, not competing primary actions.
